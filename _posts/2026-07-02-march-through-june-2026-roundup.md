## Articles & Learning

- [**VIC-II Chip Technical Reference | Zimmers.net**](https://www.zimmers.net/cbmpics/cbm/c64/vic-ii.txt)<br>
   Old-school game development and firmware have a lot in common. – François

- [**Cloning a Sennheiser BA2015 Accu Pack | brixit**](https://blog.brixit.nl/cloning-a-sennheiser-ba2015-accu-pack/)<br>
   A deep dive into reverse engineering and recreating a smart battery pack. –
   Noah

- [**Hosting a Website on an 8-bit Microcontroller | Maurycy's Projects**](https://maurycyz.com/projects/mcusite/)<br>
   A fun tour of serving a website from a tiny microcontroller. – Noah

- [**Using an LLM to Design a RISC-V CPU | arXiv**](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2603.08716)<br>
   Using an LLM to design a RISC-V CPU (pdf warning). – Noah

- [**CHERIoT First Silicon | CHERIoT**](https://cheriot.org/silicon/2026/03/04/cheriot-first-silicon.html)<br>
   The CHERIoT project has first silicon, and
   [CHERI Linux](https://cheri-linux.org/) is also looking workable. – Ash

- [**How a Broken Bike Sync Led Me to Reverse Engineering My Wahoo’s Hidden Debug Mode**](https://noahclements.com/Wahoo-Bolt-Hidden-Debug-Mode/)<br>
  Reverse engineering the Wahoo ELEMNT Bolt v3 BLE protocol unlocked a hidden
  debug mode. – Jon

- [**TLS Encrypted Client Hello (ECH) RFC**](https://www.rfc-editor.org/rfc/rfc9849.html)<br>
  TLS Encrypted Client Hello (ECH) RFC is now published. – Noah

- [**Intentional flash corruption for STM32 microcontrollers | xarantolus**](https://blog.010.one/Dont-snipe-me-in-space-intentional-flash-corruption-for-stm32-microcontrollers)<br>
  Interrupt community member Mo Ger shared that provoking flash corruption in
  order to test ECCD handling was something they recently needed for work, so
  this article proved to be quite useful for them!

- [**The overclocked timer | Mr. PY**](https://mrpy.hashnode.dev/the-overclocked-timer)<br>
  Interrupt community member Andrew Kohlsmith shared that this article was a
  well-written horror story of an overclocked timer and marathon debug session.

## Projects & Tools

- [**LightInk | Hackaday.io**](https://hackaday.io/project/205564-lightink)<br>
   Very impressive battery life on an ESP32 watch project, achieved by booting
   and running directly from RTC RAM instead of the typical flash boot path. –
   Noah

- [**Xteink X4 with Open Source Firmware | Concretedog**](https://concretedog.blogspot.com/2026/04/xteink-x4-with-open-source-firmware.html)<br>
   A small e-ink reader with open source firmware and a nice web flashing tool.
  – Noah

- [**Kindle Typewriter | Adafruit Blog**](https://blog.adafruit.com/2026/04/25/kindle-typewriter/)<br>
   More e-paper shenanigans. – Jon

- [**KiCad 10.0.0 Released | KiCad**](https://www.kicad.org/blog/2026/03/Version-10.0.0-Released/)<br>
   Kicad v10.0.0 is out- changes include hopover display for non-connected wires
  in schematic mode, very nice! – Noah

- [**Rugix delta update cost-savings calculator**](https://rugix.org/docs/ctrl/updates/delta-updates/#cost-savings-calculator)<br>
  Rugix has a neat cost-savings calculator. – Ash

- [**dangdo44/sky130_adder_4bit | GitHub**](https://github.com/dangdo44/sky130_adder_4bit)<br>
  Interrupt community member Dangdo shared a hands-on tutorial they developed
  for embedded developers interested in ASIC design. It covers the manual design
  flow of a 4-bit ripple-carry adder using the SKY130 open-source PDK and
  open-source EDA tools.
